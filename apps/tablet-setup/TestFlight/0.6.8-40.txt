PLANK AVP Relay 0.6.8

Use the matching relay package 0.6.8. It now installs both relay management and the raw drawing service. Existing app approvals and tablet bonds are preserved.

What to test:
- Discover your relay, set up a USB or Bluetooth Wacom, and confirm pen position, pressure and buttons in Test Tablet.
- Choose Use in PLANK. With the matching PLANK Vision Client build 46, approve first-time registration through Continue in Relay Setup → Allow PLANK. No tablet button sequence is required.
- In PLANK Vision, choose Automatic, Bluetooth or Network for the registered relay. Explicit Bluetooth must not silently fall back to TCP. Check drawing, pen controls and reconnect.
- Stop drawing before returning to Setup tablet operations. Confirm network controls remain usable and tablet capture transfers cleanly.
- Verify existing relay approvals survive the update and that changing a relay address does not require authorization again.

The matching PLANK Vision Client is a separate app and release. Bluetooth drawing with relay networking disabled was previously tested using a USB tablet. Simultaneous wireless Wacom input and isolated Bluetooth relay-to-headset drawing still need validation; this build does not claim that full wireless qualification.
